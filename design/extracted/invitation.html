Good to know
Questions
The wedding day
When should I arrive?+
  • Please arrive from 1:30pm. The ceremony starts at 2:00pm sharp.
  • The driveway into Glenworth Valley is long and unsealed in parts, so allow an extra 15 minutes.
  • Guests are seated at The Lookout. Ushers will guide you from the car park.
What is the schedule?+
  • 1:30pm: guests arrive at The Lookout
  • 2:00pm to 3:00pm: ceremony
  • 3:00pm to 4:30pm: photographs
  • 4:30pm to 5:30pm: cocktail hour at the Valley Events Centre
  • 5:30pm to 6:00pm: sunset surprise
  • 6:00pm to 7:00pm: reception dinner
  • 7:00pm to 11:00pm: dancing
  • 11:00pm: farewell
What should I wear?+
  • Gentlemen: tuxedo or suit.
  • Ladies: cocktail attire.
  • Soft pastels such as lilac, powder blue, sage and champagne suit the day. Colours are a guide only.
  • Please avoid pink, which is reserved for the bridesmaids, and leave white and ivory for the bride.
  • The ceremony is outdoors on grass and gravel, so block heels or flats are recommended.
What if it rains?+
  • The ceremony moves to the balcony of the Valley Events Centre, the reception venue, instead of The Lookout.
  • We will confirm any change by SMS on the morning of the wedding.
Can I take photos at the ceremony?+
  • Our ceremony is unplugged. Please keep phones and cameras away until the ceremony ends.
  • Our photographer will capture the day, and we will share the photos afterwards.
  • You are welcome to take photos from cocktail hour onwards.
Will there be drinks?+
  • Beer and wine are included at cocktail hour and the reception.
  • Cocktails are available at the bar at your own cost.
  • Please drink responsibly and plan your ride home.
Can I bring a guest or my children?+
  • Only the guests named on your invitation can RSVP. Your reply form shows how many seats are held for you.
  • If you have a question about a plus-one or children, please contact Nicholas on 0411 039 612.
Is the venue accessible?+
  • Parking is right next to the ceremony spot.
  • The Lookout is on natural ground. If you need step-free access, a seat near the aisle or any other help, tell us in your RSVP message and we will arrange it.
Is there a gift registry?+
  • Your presence is the greatest gift.
  • If you would like to give something, see the gift registry section above.
Getting there
Where is everything happening?+
  • Ceremony: The Lookout, Glenworth Valley.
  • Reception: Valley Events Centre, Glenworth Valley.
  • Address: 69 Cooks Road, Glenworth Valley NSW 2250. Get directions.
Where do I park?+
  • There is parking next to the ceremony spot.
  • At the reception venue, follow the signage to the car park.
  • Parking is free for wedding guests on Thursday.
Coming by train, taxi or Uber?+
  • The nearest station is Gosford, about 23 km away, roughly a 30 minute drive.
  • A taxi from Gosford is about $40 to $55 each way.
  • Taxis and Ubers are not always available in the valley. Book your ride there and your pick-up home in advance, and confirm the time with your driver.
  • Local cabs: Central Coast Taxis, 02 4337 3999 (direct bookings, includes maxi taxis for up to 11) or 13 10 08. Mention Glenworth Valley, 69 Cooks Road, Glenworth Valley.
Driving from Wollongong?+
  • About 2 hours (roughly 170 km) with clear roads, but please allow 3 hours on a Thursday and aim to leave by 10:30am.
  • Best route, avoiding the city: M1 Princes Motorway north, then the M5 west, M7 north, M2 east and NorthConnex onto the M1 Pacific Motorway north.
  • Take the Calga exit (Peats Ridge Road), then follow the signs onto Cooks Road to Glenworth Valley, 69 Cooks Road.
  • This route has tolls (M5, M7, M2, NorthConnex), so bring an e-tag or a pass. Check Google Maps or Live Traffic NSW on the morning.
Flying into Sydney Airport?+
  • Glenworth Valley is about 110 km from Sydney Airport. Allow 2 hours by car on a Thursday.
  • Hire car (easiest): M8 / WestConnex to the M4, then the M7 north and the M2 east. Take NorthConnex onto the M1 Pacific Motorway north, then the Calga exit (Peats Ridge Road) to Cooks Road.
  • Train: the Airport Line to Central, then the Central Coast & Newcastle Line to Gosford (about 2 hours). Then a pre-booked taxi, about 30 minutes: Central Coast Taxis 02 4337 3999.
  • Private transfer: book a car or shuttle from the airport to 69 Cooks Road, Glenworth Valley.
  • Landing on the day? Choose a flight that arrives by 10:30am, or fly in the day before and stay nearby.
How do I get home after the reception?+
  • Dancing finishes at 11:00pm.
  • Glenworth Valley is remote, so please pre-book a taxi or arrange a designated driver before the day.
  • Central Coast Taxis: 02 4337 3999. Book your 11:00pm pick-up when you book your ride there.
Will my phone work in the valley?+
  • Reception can be patchy at Glenworth Valley.
  • Before you arrive, save your directions, this invitation, your day pass and your ride booking so you can open them offline.
