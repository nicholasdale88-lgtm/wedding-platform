# 07: Owner-facing rules the UI must enforce (from months of feedback; don't regress these)

## Invitation
- Keep the **original envelope design** (theme-coloured flaps with crosshatch, wax seal, "Tap to open").
  - Detail and depth come from texture and **realistic seams** (cast shadow under the top flap plus a thin cream highlight).
  - No big radial "flash" glow on tap. The owner disliked it.
  - Rings + sparkles + falling petals are liked.
- Music must work on iPhone: prefer MP3 uploaded in the admin. YouTube is a fallback only.
- "Scroll for details" stays visible while there's more content below.

## Guests
- One **main contact** per invitation receives all texts. Any number of other people sit on the same invitation.
- Each invitation can be invited to wedding only, 30th only, or both (generally: any subset of events).
- Never use the word "household" in the UI. Use "invitation" / "guest".

## Night-before guide
- Personal to each guest. Parking directions and the **parking video at the top**.
- No countdown timer. The summary shows Arrive, Vows, Reception, Table.
- Tables shown as a dropdown. Tapping "Table 1" reveals who's at that table.
- Mention: cocktails at the Valley Events Centre. Taxis/Ubers rarely come out, so pre-book. The Irish goodbye (no farewell line).
- QR code to the photo upload page.

## Admin
- Overview must be genuinely useful: what needs doing, with one-tap buttons.
- Avoid generic card grids. Use ruled editorial sections (2px ink top rules).
- Texts must "just run": autopilot on by default, shown as one timeline. Never remind someone who has already replied.
- Dietary: per-person Entrée/Main/Dessert. Exports for the **wedding venue** and the **30th caterer**.
- Seating: round **and** long banquet tables; dance floor, DJ, bar areas; guests view-only.
- Everything editable in the admin. No code edits, no Google Sheets.
- Mobile friendly everywhere.
