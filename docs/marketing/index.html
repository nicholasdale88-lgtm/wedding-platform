Before the first ad runs

  1. Create the ads in Meta Ads Manager with the PNGs from docs/marketing/ads/ and the copy below. Meta doesn’t take SVG.
  2. Send every ad to the front page with its own link (shown in each ad’s copy) so Company portal → Promote counts sign-ups per ad. The front page has the “Open a sample invitation” button.
  3. Check that the sign-up tracking (FACEBOOK_ADS_PLAN.md section 7) is live, or judge the ads by the ?ref= counts in Promote.
  4. Film the six videos (VIDEO_SCRIPTS.md) on your phone with /demo and the app.
  5. Make the partner codes and the launch code in Company portal → Promote.
