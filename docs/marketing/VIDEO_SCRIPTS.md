# The Yes List: six short vertical videos

All six are 9:16 (1080×1920) and 7–20 seconds long, filmed on a phone. Post the same cut to Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts and Pinterest. Videos 1, 2, 3 and 4 double as Meta ads (V1–V4 in AD_COPY.md).

## Filming the whole set in one afternoon

- **Screens you need:**
  - **/demo** (the sample invitation: the wax seal, the envelope opening, the guest tapping Yes).
  - A **test wedding** in the admin with about 60 made-up guests: Guest list, Seating, Budget, Vendors, Photos.
  - Never show a real couple's guests.
- **Two ways to capture:**
  1. Screen-record the phone (iPhone: Control Centre → Screen Recording; Android: Quick Settings → Screen record).
  2. Film a hand holding a phone with a second phone, which feels more real. Use window light, not overhead light.
- **Before you record:**
  - Do Not Disturb on, full battery icon, clean wallpaper.
  - Set the phone's text size to normal.
  - Clear notifications.
- **Edit in CapCut or Instagram's editor (free):**
  - Burn the captions in, because most people watch muted.
  - Keep on-screen text inside the middle 70% of the frame. The top 250 px and the bottom 600 px get covered by app buttons.
  - Use DM Sans for the text if the editor has it (any clean sans otherwise). Use Cormorant Garamond or a light serif for the end card.
  - End card for all six: dark green `#1b2218`, the logo, "The Yes List", the price line, held for 1.5 s. The V5 ad PNG works as the end card.
- **Music:**
  - Use the platform's own commercial-safe library. On TikTok, the **Commercial Music Library** if the account is a business one.
  - Never use a chart song in anything you run as an ad.
- **Voiceover:** record on the phone in a quiet room, 20 cm from the mic. Or post with captions only. Every script works muted.
