# The Yes List: partner kit

Celebrants, planners, venues and photographers talk to couples planning a wedding every week. Partners are the cheapest paying channel we have. This kit has:
- the one-page offer;
- four first emails, one for each kind of partner;
- three follow-ups;
- a tracker.

## Before you send anything

- **Spam Act 2003 (Australia).** A business email is allowed when all of these hold:
  1. **Consent can be inferred.** The address is *published* by the business for enquiries (on its website or directory listing). There's no "no marketing" note beside it. The email is about their work.
  2. **It says who it's from.** Your name, The Yes List, your ABN and a way to contact you, in every email.
  3. **It has a working opt-out.** Every email includes one. Honour it within 5 business days (do it the same day) and keep it working for at least 30 days after sending.
- **No bought or scraped lists. Never.** One person, one email, written for them, with something specific about their business in the first line.
- **Before the first email:** make the partner's own code in **Company portal → Promote**, for example `HANNAH10` (10% off). The partner then sees exactly how many couples they've sent.
- **Their free Deluxe:** create the event for them, then switch it to Deluxe in Company portal → Weddings (marking Deluxe as received).
- **Pace:** about 5 a week, sent on Tuesdays (CONTENT_CALENDAR.md). Personal beats volume.
