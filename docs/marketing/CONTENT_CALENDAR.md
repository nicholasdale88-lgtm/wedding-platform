# The Yes List: six-week launch calendar (16 Nov 2026 → 3 Jan 2027)

The calendar runs as follows:
- **Launch week (9–15 Nov):** set up and film.
- **Weeks 1–6 (16 Nov – 27 Dec):** four posts a week.
- **One more week (28 Dec – 3 Jan):** the start of engagement season, so it isn't left empty.

Australian engagements peak from Christmas to Valentine's Day, so the library has to be ready by mid-December.

## Basics for every post

- **Assets:**
  - V1–V5 and S1–S5 are the ad images in `ads/` (use the PNG).
  - "Video 1–6" are in VIDEO_SCRIPTS.md.
- **Links:**
  - Bio link: `https://wedding-platform-nd.pages.dev/?ref=instagram` (or `?ref=tiktok`, `?ref=facebook`, `?ref=pinterest`). Swap the domain when it moves.
  - Make each link in Company portal → Promote → Campaign links, so sign-ups are counted per platform.
- **Where to post:**
  - "IG+TT+FB" means the same video on Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook Reels.
  - Add YouTube Shorts too if you like; it costs nothing.
  - Pinterest gets a static pin with a link.
- **Scheduling:**
  - Schedule in **Meta Business Suite** (Instagram + Facebook) and **TikTok's own scheduler** (both free).
  - Pinterest has its own scheduler.
  - Post around 7–8 pm AEDT on weekdays, and 10 am on weekends.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 per post. The ones listed are the starting set; swap in whichever do best.
- **Wording:**
  - Never "Are you engaged?" or "Just engaged?", even organically. Any post may later be boosted, and a boosted post is an ad.
  - Say "couples planning a wedding".
- **The launch code:**
  - **YES20** (20% off) runs only **26 Dec – 14 Feb**. Before that, no discount in any post.
  - Always write: "20% off with code YES20, ends 14 Feb".
  - Create it in Company portal → Promote before 26 Dec, and note the start date.

## The A$10/day ad test

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Tue 17 Nov | Start. One ad set, 4 ads (V1, V2, S3, V3), A$10/day, CompleteRegistration (setup in AD_COPY.md). |
| Sat 21 Nov | First look (4 days in). Change nothing unless an ad breaks a kill rule. |
| Mon 23 Nov | Kill and replace. Edits only every 3–4 days. Budget changes ≤20%. |
| Mon 30 Nov – Sun 6 Dec | Leave it running. Count purchases from Weeks 1–2 sign-ups. |
| **Mon 14 Dec** | **Decision gate.** About A$280 spent. |
| 26 Dec – 14 Feb | Scale with YES20 if the gate passed. |

**Kill an ad** (A$ spent on that ad):
- Link CTR under 0.8%, or CPC over A$2.50, after **A$15**.
- No sign-up after **A$25**.
- Cost per sign-up over A$20 after **A$40**.

**Scale an ad:**
- Its cost per sign-up is at or below break-even (A$86 × your real free→paid rate; for example, 20% paying gives A$17 per sign-up), **and** it has led to at least one purchase.
- If both hold, raise the budget 20% every 3–4 days.

**The 14 Dec gate.** At the new prices, each paying couple is worth about A$86 (about A$69 with YES20).
- Paid weddings × A$86 ≥ 70% of spend → keep it running through Christmas and scale from 26 Dec.
- 30–70% → keep A$10/day and swap in new hooks (videos 4 and 6).
- Under 30% → stop paid ads. Put the money into partners and organic posts.

**Where to look:**
- Ads Manager for spend, CTR and CPC.
- Company portal → Promote for sign-ups and paid weddings per `?ref=meta-…` link.
- Overview for free→paid.
