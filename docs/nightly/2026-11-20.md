## 1. What was simulated

- **1,000 weddings through the real API** (8 CI machines, local database), seed 20261120 (tonight's date, so new combinations from last night). Same coverage as before: sign-up, plans, 1 to 4 events (the wedding, a 30th, welcome drinks, brunch, a second day), seven time zones, 2 to 500 guests, households of 1 to 12, children, plus-ones, missing mobiles and emails, duplicate names, imports run twice, every reply path, meals, seating (round, long and square tables, over capacity, removing a table with people at it, seating rules and auto-seat), photos, texts (no placeholder left in), a vendor's proposal and booking, guests removed and put back.
- **New tonight: Setup**, on about half the weddings. The couple renames a partner (apostrophes, accents, hyphens), picks a design, writes their own wording, and sets the reply-by date: already past, today, later, or none. Checked: the guest's own page shows the new names, design and wording; the reply-by date is filled into the invitation's words ("15 January 2027"), or stays as "[RSVP date]" when none is set; the wedding date fills in where the couple hasn't written their own words. After the closing day a guest **can't change** a reply online (they're told to email), a guest replying for the **first time** still gets in, and the couple can still record a reply by hand. On the closing day itself replies are still open.
- **New tonight: the wedding party**, on about half the paid weddings. Up to 8 people given roles from the guest list (one sometimes taken away again; an unusual role with an emoji). Checked: the party list reads back exactly; "Who's who" on a guest's invitation shows exactly the party when switched on, and nothing when off; a party member's own page knows they're in the party; a role can't be given to someone from another wedding; and **another event's seating (the birthday) never shows wedding party roles**, while the wedding's seating does.
- Checked on every wedding: no server errors; counts agree on every page; wedding and birthday counts never mix; nobody seated twice; one wedding's data never visible from another; guest links only open their own invitation.

**Result:** 999 of 1,000 weddings passed first time (350,206 of 350,208 checks, about 3,000 more than last night from the new steps), with no server errors. The one failure was a real bug (glitch 1). After the fix: **1,000 of 1,000 passed** (350,220 checks), no server errors.
