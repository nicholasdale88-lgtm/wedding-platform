Weddings run across time zones, with extra events (a 30th, welcome drinks, a brunch).
- **New tonight (step 12g): the message board and song requests**, from guests' own links. Neither was covered before.
  - With "approve first" on, a new post is hidden from other guests until the couple shows it. Its writer still sees it, marked as waiting.
  - The couple's "waiting" count is right.
  - Hearts toggle, and a second tap takes one back. The count agrees for the couple.
  - A reply sits under its post.
  - A guest can't remove someone else's post. They can remove their own, and its replies go with it.
