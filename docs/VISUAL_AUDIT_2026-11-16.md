# Visual audit: 16 November 2026

Every public page and every sample screen was checked on the live site at phone (390px), tablet (768px) and desktop (1280px) width.

**How each page was checked:**
- **Automatic checks** for sideways scrolling, anything running off the screen, broken images, text cut off inside its box, words sitting on top of other words, and tap targets smaller than a finger on phones.
- **By eye** for the invitation, the envelope, the Designs page and the sample couple app.

## What was checked

| Area | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| Sales | Front page, Designs, Try, Templates, Privacy, Terms, Contact |
| Vendors | Directory, Join, a listing page, and the sample vendor account (Your couples, Recommend us, Enquiries, Your listing) |
| Sample couple app | Overview, Guests, Dietary, Seating, Setup, Help, Messages, Texts, Photos, Wedding party, On the day, Planner |
| Guests | The envelope (light and dark), the opened invitation (every section), "not found" links |
| Sign-in | Couple admin, Company portal, Reset passcode |

## Fixed today

**Envelope and designs:**

1. **The paper envelope** was greyish beige. It also had a torn flap edge, a graph-paper grid and streaks like brushed metal. It is now:
   - warm ivory paper with a soft, natural grain
   - a crisp folded flap with a soft shadow
   - a fine gold-foil rule along the flap, ending in a dot before the sprig

   The torn edge now appears only when a couple picks "Handmade cotton (torn edge)".
2. **Dark envelopes** (navy, burgundy, forest):
   - The flap stays rich instead of washing out to grey.
   - The white blotches are gone.
   - "Tap to open" is now light, so it can be read. It was navy on navy.
3. **Garden-look invitation cards** had a regular dot grid that read as graph paper. They now have an irregular paper grain lit from the top.
4. **Designs page:** each design's tile is now a little envelope in its own colours (flap, foil rule, seal) instead of a flat swatch.
5. **Front page envelope pictures:**
   - The envelope's own "Tap to open" and names no longer sit under the card captions. They overlapped on phones.
   - The crosshatch weave is replaced with the same soft grain as the invitation's envelope.

**Layout and tapping:**

6. **Vendor account, Your couples, on a phone:** the couple cards ran off the right edge of the screen. They now fit.
7. **Tap targets on phones:**
   - Links in page headers and footers (Sign in, Privacy, Terms and the rest) are now at least 40px tall.
   - Tick boxes and radio buttons are now 20px. They were 13px.
   - The small "All texts", "Assign tasks" and "Guest list" links on the overview cards have a bigger tap area.
8. **Sample couple app on a phone:** the sample bar at the bottom was two rows and covered about 110px of every screen. It is now one row ("←", the plan switch, "Start yours").

**Wording:**

9. **A dead invitation link** now tells the guest what to do: "Each invitation link is personal. Open the one sent to you by text or email, or ask the couple to send it again." It still says nothing that leads to the admin.
10. **Brand name in the default FAQ:** "Uber" became "rideshare" in the default questions ("Coming by train, taxi or rideshare?") and the late-night transport answer. Your own wedding's wording was left as you wrote it.

## Checked and fine

- No page scrolls sideways at any width.
- No broken images anywhere checked.
- The opened sample invitation is clean at phone and desktop width. That covers the countdown, the day, the timeline, the dress code, accommodation, RSVP and the questions.
- Sign-in, reset passcode and the vendor pages are clean.
- Sample Setup (mood boards, venue photo, preview) works at every width.

## Small things left as they are

- **Small text links (about 21px tall):** "Forgot your passcode?", "New here? Start your wedding" and "Back to the directory" on the sign-in pages. They're readable and work. Making them taller would add space on calm pages.
- **Seating (sample) "The rules (7 on)" toggle** is 20px tall. It's a text toggle beside bigger buttons.
- **Front page phone mock-ups** use 9 to 10px text on purpose (they are pictures of a phone).

## Not covered

- **The company portal and your own wedding's admin** need your sign-in. Only their sign-in pages were checked. The sample screens and Company → Preview share the same parts.
- **Guest pages that need a real guest link:** the photo page, the night-before guide and the wedding party pages. Their shared parts (cards, buttons, the not-found page) were covered through the sample invitation.
- **Phone-size screenshots:** the browser panel cut off the right edge, so phone layouts were judged from the measured checks. Desktop was also checked by eye.
