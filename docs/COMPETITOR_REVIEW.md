# Competitor review and gap analysis (October 2026)

Scope: Zola, The Knot/WeddingWire, Joy, Minted, Greenvelope, Paperless Post, RSVPify, Evite, Riley & Grey, Appy Couple, Prismm (AllSeated), Easy Weddings, Event Invite Hub (AU), plus couples' complaints. Prices are USD unless marked A$. Most sources are vendor or competitor marketing pages. Treat prices as indicative and re-check them before you publish sales copy.

## 2. What couples complain about
- **RSVPs vanish or duplicate** (The Knot, Zola), so caterer counts are wrong ([unstar roundup](https://unstar.app/blog/zola-the-knot-joy-weddingwire-minted-wedding-planning-apps-ranked-2026)). Zola caps an invitation at 1 RSVP unless each guest is entered individually ([WeddingWire](https://www.weddingwire.com/wedding-forums/zola-issues-advice-appreciated/275e8fe3908db9e7.html)).
- **Older guests are confused** by links and app-install prompts (Joy) ([unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/zola-the-knot-joy-weddingwire-minted-wedding-planning-apps-ranked-2026)). Couples fall back to phone calls and entering replies by hand ([WeddingWire](https://www.weddingwire.com/wedding-forums/online-rsvp-vs-traditional/4295f8d27e005c1b.html)).
- **Chasing non-responders** by text the day after the deadline is the norm ([WeddingWire](https://www.weddingwire.com/wedding-forums/rsvp-deadline/6da18c7368f9a1e9.html), [Easy Weddings](https://www.easyweddings.com.au/articles/guests-who-dont-rsvp/)).
- **Plus-one abuse.** Guests claim extra seats through free-text note fields ([r/weddingshaming](https://reddit.fabiomanganiello.com/r/weddingshaming/comments/wvq1at/uninvited_guests_attempting_to_rsvp/ilk56lt/?context=3)).
- **Hidden fees and upsells**: Zola's 2.5–2.9% cash-fund fee, Joy's paid domain and RSVP logic appearing mid-setup, and Knot vendor spam (15+ DJs in 48 h) ([unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/zola-the-knot-joy-weddingwire-minted-wedding-planning-apps-ranked-2026)).
- **Mobile editing bugs.** Knot edits made on iPhone revert, and WeddingWire crashes on large photo uploads (same source).

## 3. Gap analysis

**Competitors have it; we don't plan it yet:** save-the-date send (Greenvelope, Joy, Appy Couple); contact/mobile collector form (Joy); "opened, not replied" tracking (Greenvelope); auto-seat (Joy AI, Prismm); live reception photo slideshow (Joy); guestbook/toasts (Appy Couple); calendar (.ics) after RSVP (RSVPify); QR code for paper invites (Greenvelope, RSVPify); site password (Joy, Minted); venue/planner share of the floor plan (Prismm); email delivery channel (all of them); integrated cash fund (Zola, Joy); day-of QR check-in (RSVPify).

**Our differentiators (sales copy):**
1. **Reply YES or NO by text.** Two-way SMS RSVPs with autopilot reminders that never chase someone who has already replied. None of the big platforms does this natively.
2. **Personal links, no lookup, no app, no account.** Guests open their link and are greeted by name. This answers the older-guest and Joy-app complaints.
3. **A night-before personal guide**: their table, tablemates, meal, BOM weather, parking video, taxis and run sheet. No competitor found offers it.
4. **Multi-event invitations with per-guest visibility** (wedding + birthday ride). Each guest sees only what they're invited to.
5. **Guest photos from the browser** with filters, frames and text, then one-click zip of originals. Joy needs its app.
6. **Built for Australia**: BOM weather, Spam Act-compliant STOP, a BSB gift-account card with no registry fees, local taxis, and A$ one-off pricing.
7. **No ads, no vendor spam, no registry cut.** Works offline. Caterer-ready exports default to "Set menu".

## 4. Recommendations: value vs effort

All items fit the free tier and locked design unless they are flagged otherwise. "New screen" means it reuses the admin components.

| # | Improvement | Why (source) | Effort | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Use Google sign-in + SMS one-time code instead, or verify whether Cloudflare's own email sending is free before relying on it. | Every competitor emails | M | **Out of budget if it needs a paid email provider** |

**Free-tier risks to watch:**
- **R2's 10 GB free storage.** Storing both the original and the edited copy of every photo, across many tenants, will exceed it. Set a per-wedding photo quota (for example 3 GB). Delete originals 90 days after the last event, once the couple has downloaded the zip, or sell a "Photo keep" add-on.
- **SMS credits are a real cost.** Gate sending behind a prepaid balance.

## 5. Pricing and packaging (AU)

Benchmarks: Greenvelope's 100-guest tier costs about A$150 for invitations alone, with no seating or guide. Event Invite Hub charges A$99–200. Print costs A$600–1,500 per 100 invitations. Joy is free but upsells.

- **Free to build**: the full editor, preview and a test send to yourself, as docs/13 plans. Matches Joy's free entry and removes the "upsell mid-setup" complaint because the price is shown up front.
- **Essentials A$89** (invite, RSVP, personal links, exports, contact collector). **Complete A$189** (adds SMS autopilot, night-before guide, seating, photos, slideshow, 300 SMS credits included). Run a launch price of A$149 for Complete while building reviews.
- **Upgrade** Essentials to Complete for the difference. **Extend** for A$29 per 6 months after the 90-day read-only window.
- **SMS packs**: our cost is about 1.6–4¢ ex GST ([Mobile Message pricing](https://www.mobilemessage.com.au/pricing)). Sell 500 credits for A$25 and 1,500 for A$59 (about 4–5¢ each). A 100-invitation wedding uses about 500–800 credits (invite, 2 reminders, receipt, night-before).
- **Partner codes**: Greenvelope runs co-branded partner subdomains with 20% off ([example](https://ashandstone.greenvelope.com/resources/how-much-does-greenvelope-cost)). Copy this with Stripe promotion codes for AU planners and venues (S effort; super-admin screen).
- **Messaging**: "One price, no ads, no registry fees, guests reply by text." Compare it against the cost of printed invitations, not against free US sites.
