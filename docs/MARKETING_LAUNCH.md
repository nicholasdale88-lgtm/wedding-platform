# The Yes List: launch campaign on a small budget

The aim for the first 90 days is to get the first 100 paying weddings. That should cost almost nothing in ads: spend time, not money, until we know what turns a visitor into a paying couple.

## The numbers that decide whether ads pay

| | Essentials | Deluxe |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $28 | $35 |
| Stripe fee (1.7% + 30c) | about 78c | about 90c |
| **What we keep** | **about $27.20** | **about $34.10** |

The rough maths for a cold paid ad (Meta or Google) aimed at engaged couples in Australia:
- A click costs about $1 to $3.
- Perhaps 5–10% of visitors sign up free.
- Perhaps 20–30% of free weddings upgrade.

That is about **$40 to $150 to win one paying couple**, against $27 to $34 earned. **Cold ads lose money at our price.** They only pay once we know our real sign-up and upgrade rates and can aim very tightly.

What does pay at our price:
- free and organic reach;
- referrals;
- every free invitation carrying "Made with The Yes List" to around 100 guests.

So the budget plan is:
- **$0 in month one.**
- At most **$50–$100 as a test** in month two, only on the one post that did best organically, boosted to engaged couples within 50 km of capital cities.
- Stop any ad that costs more than $25 per paying wedding.

## The built-in growth loop (already live)

- **Free-plan invitations carry "Made with The Yes List · make your own"**, which links to the front page with that wedding counted as the referrer. One free wedding with 100 guests puts the app in front of about 100 people, and some of them are engaged.
- **Promo codes** live in Company portal → Promote. Make one per partner (for example CELEBRANT10), so you can see who sends couples.
- **Campaign links** come from Promote → "Campaign links": use `?ref=instagram`, `?ref=tiktok`, `?ref=<venue>`. Sign-ups and paid weddings are counted for each link.
- **Ready-to-post captions** with your link and code filled in are in Promote → "Ready to post".

## The campaign: "The invitation they'll actually open"

One idea, used everywhere: **the envelope opening on a phone**, and the guest list filling itself in. It is visual, short, and it is what nobody else at our price shows.

### Content (free; film it on a phone with the sample invitation at /demo)

1. **The open** (7 seconds): a thumb taps the wax seal, the envelope opens, the card rises, and a guest taps Yes. Text on screen: "Your guests reply in one tap." End card: "$28. Once. The Yes List."
2. **POV: you stopped chasing RSVPs**: a split screen with a messy spreadsheet on one side and the guest list filling in on the other.
3. **"What we paid vs what you'll pay"**: the cost bars from the front page, read out loud. Printed invitations at $400–$800 against $28.
4. **Seating in a second**: drag guests, press auto-seat, and every table fills while keeping the rules.
5. **Behind the scenes**: "We built this for our own wedding." Real, warm, and people share founder stories.

Post the same video to TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts and Pinterest (Idea Pins). Aim for 3 or 4 posts a week. Use the hashtags #weddingplanning #engaged #weddinginvitations #australianwedding #bride2027 #weddinghacks.

### Where engaged couples already are (all free)

- **Facebook groups:** wedding planning, "Brides of Sydney / Melbourne / Brisbane / Perth", budget weddings. Read each group's rules first. Answer questions helpfully, and share the sample invitation only when someone asks about invitations or RSVPs.
- **Reddit:** r/weddingplanning and r/AustralianWeddings, with the same rule: help first.
- **Pinterest:** pin the cost comparison, the seating plan and invitation designs. Pinterest is where Australian couples plan, and pins last for years.
- **Your own wedding:** your guests see the invitation, your wedding party uses the portal, and the 30th invitation is another showcase.

### Partners: the cheapest paying channel

Celebrants, small venues, photographers and planners talk to engaged couples every week.
- Offer each one a code: their couples get 10% off, and the partner gets free Deluxe for their own events.
- List them free in the vendor directory, which helps them too.
- Start with five you can message personally this week.

### Timing

- In Australia, engagements peak from **Christmas to Valentine's Day**. Have the content library ready by mid-December, and run a launch offer (code **YES20**, 20% off) from Boxing Day to 14 February.
- Wedding expos (February–March) are paid, so skip them for now. Their Facebook event pages are free to post in.

## Week by week for the first month

| Week | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Set up the free accounts: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook page, Pinterest business, Google Business Profile, Search Console. Film videos 1 and 2. Turn on the front-page banner with the launch offer. |
| 2 | Post 3 times. Join 5 Facebook groups and help in them daily. Message 5 local celebrants or venues with a partner code. |
| 3 | Post 3–4 times. Film videos 3 and 4. In Company portal → Overview and Promote, see which links bring sign-ups. |
| 4 | Double down on the best video. Ask the first paying couples for a one-line review to put on the front page. Decide whether the $50 boost test is worth it. |

## What to measure (Company portal → Overview and Promote)

- **Sign-ups per week**, and where they came from (`?ref=`).
- **Free → paid rate.** Under 15% means look at the upgrade moment. Over 30% means it is worth spending more on getting sign-ups.
- **Cost per paying wedding** for anything paid: keep it **under $25**.
- **Feedback** (the Feedback tab): what couples ask for becomes the next update. Tell them when it ships, because that makes fans.
